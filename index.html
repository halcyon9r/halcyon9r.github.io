Helionyx Backup

A private, single-user tool. It uses rclone to copy the owner's server backups into the owner's own Google Drive, check them, and remove old ones.

It is not offered to the public. See the privacy policy.

Helionyx Backup - Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy - Helionyx Backup

Helionyx Backup is used only by its owner, on the owner's own server.

It accesses only the owner's Google Drive, and only to upload backup files, verify them and delete old backups.

It does not collect, store, sell or share data about anyone. Data from Google APIs is never transferred to third parties or used for advertising.
